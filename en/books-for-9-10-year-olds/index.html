Books for 9 and 10 year olds

Mysteries and imaginative worlds can encourage children to return to a book over several days. Start with their interests rather than age alone.

Follow their interests

Try a digital adventure for a gaming fan or a mystery for a reader who likes discussing clues.

Talk about the story

Ask what might happen next without turning reading into a test.
