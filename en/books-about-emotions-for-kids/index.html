Children’s books about emotions and fears

Fiction can help families talk about feelings without turning every story into a lesson or replacing professional support.

A school story

Batalla con sus miedos is aimed at ages 10–12 and portrays feelings as characters in Andaira’s mind. The available edition is in Spanish.

Read and talk

Ask which character surprised the reader and listen without demanding personal answers.

Stories to explore

Andaira Series · Part 1

Emotions · Ages 10–12 · Spanish edition

Batalla con sus miedos

At school, Andaira faces nerves, words that will not come out and changes in her friendship. In her mind live Panzotron, fear; Oso Perezzz, laziness; Tristán, sadness; and Blanqui, joy. A story for talking about emotions together as a family.
