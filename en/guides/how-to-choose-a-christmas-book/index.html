How to choose a Christmas book for children

Match a festive book to the child’s interests, reading confidence and preferred language. A suitable story can become part of a shared holiday routine.

Three things to check

Look at the theme, reading difficulty and format. Share the first pages with younger readers; let older children compare summaries.

Before giving a book

Check the edition language, availability and delivery. A story about magic and mystery may suit a child who enjoys imaginative adventures.
