Alberto López was born in Málaga and spends his free time writing. Andairablox became a way to connect with his daughter Ariadna, to whom he dedicated the collection.

That connection grew into a universe of adventures starring Andaira, a curious girl exploring creativity, mystery and the digital world.

Alberto publishes as ALMCREATOR. Alongside writing, he offers book formatting, cover design and help preparing books for publication on Amazon.
