Do you have to start with book one? Choosing a standalone adventure

Alberto López

A numbered book series is not always one continuous story. Here is how to choose a first read without buying the whole set.

Check whether the plot continues

Read the description and see whether the story depends on earlier events. A continuous saga may carry unresolved conflicts across volumes; a standalone adventure introduces a new episode that can be enjoyed on its own.

Choose by interest rather than number

The four AndairaBlox books can be purchased and read in any order. Pick the theme that appeals most: YouTube, vacation adventures, Halloween or Christmas. Gamer and Andaira are separate collections.

Start with one gift

Preview a few pages and check the edition language before ordering. There is no need to purchase a complete set. After finishing, ask the child which setting or theme they want to explore next.
