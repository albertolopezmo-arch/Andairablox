7 Christmas reading activities for winter break

Alberto López

Try these low-prep activities with any Christmas story during school break.

Three ways to begin

1. Hunt for clues on the cover. 2. Ask a question inspired by the title. 3. Create a cozy reading spot and let each child imagine a character who might appear. No supplies beyond paper and pencils are needed.

Three ways to explore

4. Sketch a map of the story world. 5. Act out a conversation using different voices. 6. Write a postcard from a character’s point of view, using original words rather than copying the book.

One playful ending

7. Invent a different ending and compare it with the real one after finishing. Choose one activity at a time; skip any that feels like schoolwork. Kids can reuse their favorites with another holiday book.
