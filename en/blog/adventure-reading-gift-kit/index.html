Make an adventure reading gift kit for a young reader

Alberto López

Turn an adventure book into a creative gift using inexpensive supplies you may already own.

Choose a setting

Ask whether the child enjoys camping trips, beaches or mysteries. Pick a story that fits and preview a few pages before purchasing. Reuse a small box rather than buying special packaging.

Add three simple tools

Pack a notebook for clues, a pencil and a blank map. Include a mission card: draw the character’s route or invent a new location. Do not spoil the ending in the clues.

Plan a real-world adventure

Offer a park outing or library trip to collect story ideas. The kit works for birthdays or the holidays. Let the young reader choose which activities to try and when to open the book.
