From gaming to reading: a seven-day challenge for kids

Alberto López

Use a child’s interest in games as a bridge to stories, without treating screen time and books as enemies.

Days 1 and 2: choose a quest

Browse a mystery or adventure book together and preview a few pages. Make a character card with one strength and one challenge. The book does not have to be an official Roblox or Minecraft title to spark interest.

Days 3 through 5: unlock clues

Read a short section each day. On paper, record one clue, one decision and one unanswered question. On day five, invent an optional side quest for the character.

Days 6 and 7: design a level

Draw a map with an obstacle and two possible routes. Explain how the main character could solve it. End by choosing whether to keep reading or switch books. Keep the challenge optional instead of making gaming time a reward for pages read.
