Start a family Christmas book club for ages 7–12

Alberto López

Make holiday break a chance to share a chapter book without turning reading into homework.

Plan three cozy meetings

Pick three evenings during winter break, after dinner or before bedtime. Let each child choose a seat and a snack. Read for about fifteen minutes; stop at a natural break even if you have not finished the chapter.

Make predictions

Ask what a character might do after receiving a mysterious letter. Write two predictions on a sticky note and revisit them at the next meeting. Younger siblings can draw their guesses while older readers explain theirs.

Build a tradition

Finish with a homemade bookmark showing a favorite scene. Save it for next December. If a family member cannot join, invite them to share a short voice message about the story.
