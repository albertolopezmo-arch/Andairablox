Christmas gifts for 10-year-olds: five ways to make a book special

Alberto López

A book gift can feel personal without buying a big bundle or guessing what a child will like.

Make the wrapping part of the fun

First, tuck a handwritten clue about the story inside the wrapping. Second, pair the book with a bookmark you made together. A personal detail can matter more than an expensive accessory.

Connect it to their interests

Third, add a small notebook for designing characters or drawing maps. Fourth, give a homemade coupon for a trip to the public library or a bookstore where the child can choose the next read.

Give time as well as a book

Fifth, offer a cozy read-aloud evening with a favorite snack. Check a sample page, the edition language, and the delivery date before ordering. An independent story is a useful option when you do not know which series volumes the child owns.
