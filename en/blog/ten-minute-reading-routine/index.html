A ten-minute reading routine without a daily battle

Alberto López

A short, flexible routine can make room for books in busy school weeks.

Pick a realistic time

Try after a snack or just before bed, when nobody is rushing. Keep two choices nearby and let your child decide. On an exhausting day, move the session or offer to read a passage aloud yourself.

Vary the way you read

Trade paragraphs one day and offer independent reading the next. Help with unfamiliar words without stopping every sentence. Ask about an interesting detail rather than requiring a full summary.

Review after one week

Notice which time felt relaxed and which story held their interest. Avoid comparing page counts. If ongoing difficulty is making reading frustrating, ask the child’s teacher about appropriate support.
