READING GUIDES · ANDAIRABLOX

Books for reluctant readers

When a child is reluctant to read, begin with a favourite interest rather than a target number of pages. Different readers need different stories.

Start with interests

Offer a Halloween mystery, a digital adventure or a Christmas story. Try available samples together.

Reduce pressure

Take turns reading briefly and allow a change of book. Ask teachers for advice if difficulties persist.
