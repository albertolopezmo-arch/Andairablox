BEFORE YOU OPEN THE BOOK

What you want to know.

What is Trick or Treat about?

Andaira and her friends are getting ready for Halloween, but strange events disrupt the neighbourhood’s plans. An adventure to uncover what lies behind the most mysterious night of the year.

Who is it for?

It belongs to the Andairablox universe, aimed at children aged 7–12. The right choice also depends on reading level and interests; check the edition’s Amazon listing before buying.

Is it only for Halloween?

Halloween is the setting, but you can enjoy Andaira and her friends’ mystery at any time of year.
